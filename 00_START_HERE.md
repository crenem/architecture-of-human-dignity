# Architecture of Human Dignity
## A living research laboratory in the service of persons

**The person is the end. Institutions, markets, capital, and artificial intelligence are instruments.**

This vault asks a design question: **What would our economic, civic, organizational, and technological institutions look like if human dignity and the common good were architectural requirements rather than ceremonial aspirations?**

### Four distinct layers
1. **The tradition:** [[sources/CATHOLIC_SOCIAL_TEACHING|Catholic social teaching]] and [[sources/MAGNIFICA_HUMANITAS_2026|Magnifica Humanitas (2026)]]; primary-source authority belongs to the original documents, not our commentary.
2. **The author's constructive work:** [[works/AN_ECONOMY_FOR_THE_COMMON_GOOD|An Economy for the Common Good]], [[works/THIRTEEN_LEON|13 LEON]], [[works/CONSTITUTION_OF_THE_COMMON_GOOD|Constitution of the Common Good]], and developing work on [[works/THE_PUBLIC_GOOD|The Public Good]] and [[works/LEVINAS_BUSINESS_ETHICS|Lévinasian business ethics]].
3. **The applied architecture:** [[PROJECT_AXIOMS|the shared axioms]], [[ai/ARCHITECTURE|AI architecture]], [[ai/DECISION_PROTOCOL|decision protocol]], and [[ai/REQUIREMENTS|testable requirements]].
4. **Accountability:** [[evaluations/README|evaluation program]], [[governance/GOVERNANCE|governance]], and [[LICENSE|HDPL v0.1 public-review draft]].

### Orientation
Dignity is not a score. Solidarity is not paternalism. Subsidiarity is not abandonment. The common good is not the sum of profitable transactions. Efficiency is a means, not the purpose. Good intentions do not substitute for evidence, due process, or independent review.

**Reading discipline:** Every assertion should identify whether it is (D) Catholic primary text, (A) the author's original proposal, (H) a project hypothesis, (E) empirical observation, or (L) a provision of the experimental license. These categories must not be conflated.

The project is open to criticism and participation from people of any religion or none. It claims neither Church endorsement nor legal certification. This repository is an **Obsidian-compatible research vault** and an evolving framework, not a deployed AI product.

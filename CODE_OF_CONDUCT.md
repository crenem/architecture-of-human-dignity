# Code of Conduct
## Dignity is not contingent on agreement

This is a research and design community. Participation is open across religious beliefs, nonbelief, backgrounds, disabilities and legitimate differences in philosophical opinion.

### Expected conduct
Engage with evidence and the strongest good-faith objections. Preserve human dignity in disagreement. Respect privacy and consent. Make room for accessibility needs. Disclose material conflicts of interest. Seek correction and fair remedy when harm occurs.

### Unacceptable conduct
Threats, harassment, targeted degrading attacks, doxxing, retaliation against good-faith concerns, deliberate fabrication or misattribution of sources, unauthorized publication of sensitive records, and coercion of religious belief.

### Enforcement principles
Moderation should be proportionate, evidence-based, documented and reviewable. Criticism of Catholic theology, this project's economics or project leadership is **not itself misconduct**. The accused should ordinarily receive notice and an opportunity to respond consistent with safety. Serious allegations may require independent review. Confidentiality must be respected.

### Limits
Publishing this code does not create a staffed moderation office, independent appeals body or legally binding adjudication process. Implement such structures before claiming they operate.

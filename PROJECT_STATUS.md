# Project Status
## Research Edition 0.1 — initial integrated publication

### Scope completed in this research edition
- Foundational [[FOUNDATIONAL_PRINCIPLES|Charter]], a traceable [[PROJECT_AXIOMS|axiom set]] and explicit freedom-of-conscience safeguards.
- **Verified official source** [[sources/MAGNIFICA_HUMANITAS_2026|*Magnifica Humanitas* (Leo XIV, May 2026)]] alongside an introductory [[sources/CATHOLIC_SOCIAL_TEACHING|doctrinal reading map]].
- Authorial source notes on [[works/AN_ECONOMY_FOR_THE_COMMON_GOOD|An Economy for the Common Good]], [[works/THIRTEEN_LEON|13 LEON]], and the [[works/CONSTITUTION_OF_THE_COMMON_GOOD|Constitution of the Common Good]], preserving the works' separate identities.
- Applications of the author's professional ethical work on [[works/ETHICS_OF_EFFICIENCY|the ethics of efficiency]], [[works/TRAUMA_INFORMED_LEADERSHIP|trauma-informed leadership]] and [[governance/CHANGE_CONTROL|continuous quality improvement]].
- [[ai/ARCHITECTURE|Reference architecture]], [[ai/DECISION_PROTOCOL|decision protocol]], numbered [[ai/REQUIREMENTS|requirements]], threat modeling, and a design-level reference prompt.
- [[evaluations/CASE_LIBRARY|Synthetic ethical cases]], [[evaluations/RUBRIC|non-aggregate rubric]], and simple Python/manual evaluation tooling.
- Obsidian [[00_START_HERE|home]], graph-friendly links and [[diagrams/SYSTEMS_MAP|Mermaid diagrams]].
- Draft governance, contributor rules, research ethics, stewardship, and [[LICENSE|experimental ethical-source license]].

### Not completed or claimed
- **No legally approved HDPL** or determination of enforceability or OSI-approved open-source status.
- **No implemented or field-tested ethical AI engine**, product, audit service or certification body.
- **No validated mathematical economic model** proving any specific investor return ceiling or universal policy formula.
- **No full public release of the author's unpublished manuscripts** without explicit authorization.
- **No actual human-subject pilot or independently replicated model benchmark**; test cases are synthetic and remain unscored unless a reviewer supplies evidence.
- **No assertion that the project has papal endorsement**; cited Church documents are independent primary sources.
- **Some original concept notes** (including The Public Good, Ubuntu Commons and planetary interdependence) have not been verified against a complete original manuscript. They are labeled provisional.

### What the automation validates
The GitHub workflow checks Markdown links and executes unit tests for evaluation helper scripts. **A passing workflow shows software/tooling integrity at that limited scope; it is not a judgment about the doctrine, legal license, or ethics of a future AI deployment.**

### Release review questions
Can an independent reader:
1. Find sources and identify who wrote them?
2. Distinguish Catholic doctrine from the author's original economic proposals and from the project's engineering hypotheses?
3. Understand how the project would protect someone wrongly denied a service?
4. Run the simple tools and interpret missing evidence as missing—not as a passing result?
5. Identify unresolved legal questions and critique the design without being required to share a religion?

If any answer is no, file a documented improvement request rather than treating the edition as finished.

**Next research priorities:** detailed paragraph-by-paragraph doctrinal verification; further analysis of original full manuscripts and exact formulas; independent review of the economics and license; validated test-case scoring; and small, lawful human-supervised pilots.

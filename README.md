# Architecture of Human Dignity

**An open research initiative on how economic, institutional, and technological power can serve persons and communities.**

The project explores the intersection of Catholic social teaching, human dignity, the common good, democratic governance, worker ownership, restorative justice, and accountable artificial intelligence. It draws on original works by **Jason Seaux**, including *An Economy for the Common Good*, *13 LEON Corporation — For the Common Good of Humankind*, and *The Constitution of the Common Good*.

> An economy is a tool. Human beings are the end for which it exists. — *An Economy for the Common Good* (project epigraph)

## Intellectual boundaries

1. **Catholic social doctrine** supplies a moral and philosophical tradition; cite its primary texts and distinguish interpretation from doctrine.
2. **Original authored frameworks** propose particular institutions and economic mechanisms; they are not official Catholic prescriptions.
3. **AI architecture and evaluations** are tentative engineering applications, subject to empirical testing and community review.
4. **Legal terms** are only those in LICENSE.md. Philosophical statements elsewhere do not silently expand legal obligations.

No original source manuscripts are reproduced in full here. Publication and rights clearance for any underlying documents must be addressed separately. References to works in the research index are not an assertion that their full text has been published.

## Status

**Early research edition.** The charter and HDPL are drafts. The repository does not claim Vatican approval, professional certification, tested AI safety guarantees, or legal validation of the proposed license.

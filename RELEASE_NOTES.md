# Release Notes — Research Edition 0.1
## Architecture of Human Dignity

**Release type:** Integrated research and prototype specification. **Not:** legally certified license, OSI open-source approval, functioning AI model, commercial product, ecclesial endorsement or validated safety benchmark.

### What this edition establishes
It brings the author's related work into a **source-grounded, distinct but interconnected structure** instead of merging works into an anonymous manifesto. The moral thesis—institutions exist to serve persons rather than persons existing to serve systems—is developed at philosophical, economic, constitutional, service-delivery and AI-engineering levels.

### Intellectual foundations
- [[PROJECT_AXIOMS|Project axioms]] clarify dignity, agency, solidarity, non-domination, humility, care and accountability.
- [[sources/MAGNIFICA_HUMANITAS_2026|*Magnifica Humanitas* (2026)]] provides a verified direct contemporary doctrinal reference concerning AI and the common good.
- Original work summaries preserve the independent identities of [[works/AN_ECONOMY_FOR_THE_COMMON_GOOD|the economic manuscript]], [[works/THIRTEEN_LEON|13 LEON]], [[works/CONSTITUTION_OF_THE_COMMON_GOOD|the proposed Constitution]], and [[works/ETHICS_OF_EFFICIENCY|the ethics of efficiency]].

### Engineering and quality
- [[ai/ARCHITECTURE|Socio-technical reference architecture]]; [[ai/REQUIREMENTS|18 testable draft requirements]]; [[ai/DECISION_PROTOCOL|ten-step ethical decision process]].
- [[evaluations/CASE_LIBRARY|10 synthetic domain case notes]] and [[evaluations/RUBRIC|8 independent assessment dimensions]] with separate severe-failure flags.
- Five structured JSON case samples, manual evaluation report script, Obsidian link linter, unit tests and GitHub Actions validation.
- Governance controls that require actual **corrective-action closure**, not simply a completed audit report.

### Readable as a knowledge vault
The files remain plain Markdown. Open the repository as an Obsidian vault and navigate from [[00_START_HERE|home]]. Mermaid diagrams and a concept map visualize dependencies.

### Known gaps
See [[PROJECT_STATUS|status]] and [[research/SOURCE_REGISTER|source register]]. The HDPL 0.1 is only a legal research draft; no live AI application or human-participant study has been built. Third-party copyright, data protection and professional review remain necessary.

### Method
All significant claims should be marked as **doctrine / authorial work / research hypothesis / empirical observation / legal statement**, rather than permitting the project to manufacture authority by blending them.

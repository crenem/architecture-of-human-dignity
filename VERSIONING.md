# Versioning and Release Discipline

### What the version numbers mean
**Research edition:** changes to a living body of work. Draft 0.x indicates active revision.

**HDPL license version:** legal text is separate from the research edition. A license version governs copies offered with that version; never imply a newer page retroactively rewrites an older grant.

**Evaluation case version:** the prompt, factual premises, expected behaviors and rubric version must be frozen so comparisons remain interpretable.

**Implementation software version:** only applies if functioning software is actually written and tested. Documentation is not proof of deployment.

### Change categories
Editorial (no substantive claims altered); substantive scholarship (sources/interpretation); governance (rights and decision authority); technical requirements; legal license changes; benchmark revision. Give explicit reasons and migration notes for rights-critical changes.

### Proposed milestones
- **0.1 Research Edition:** source-grounded charter and original works, AI requirements, case library and governance drafts.
- **0.2 Research Edition:** independent source verification and first reproducible benchmark outcomes.
- **1.0 Research Edition:** publishable scholarship after peer review and critical gap resolution.

No milestone is promised until verified. **The HDPL remains a public-review draft regardless of the research edition number.**

### Audit rule
Every release note should include what is verified, untested, contradicted, withheld for rights, and awaiting independent review.

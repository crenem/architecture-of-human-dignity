# Common-Good Enterprise Scorecard
## Make the conditions of flourishing visible without reducing them to one grade

A conventional business dashboard shows revenue, margin, cost and throughput. These matter: without viability, commitments cannot be sustained. But **financial success cannot substitute for justice**. This scorecard makes operational, social and ecological dimensions visible as a **set of measures**, not an aggregate moral rating.

| Domain | Candidate leading measures | Candidate outcome measures | Potential blind spot |
|---|---|---|---|
| Economic viability | Liquidity forecast, quality controls, reinvestment allocation | Solvency, enterprise survival, appropriate reserves | Survival can be achieved through exploitative labor |
| Worker dignity | Accommodation response, safe grievance channels, worker voice, training access | Injuries, turnover, pay adequacy, appeal reversals | Low reported grievances may mean fear |
| Democratic governance | Accessible elections, financial literacy, delegated authority map | Member participation, documented changes from dissent | Voting can still oppress minorities |
| Equity and access | Translation, disability access, outreach resources | Disaggregated access and error burdens | Averaging can hide excluded groups |
| Customers and communities | Consent, feedback follow-up, supplier ethics | Trust, useful service outcomes, community benefits | Satisfaction surveys may omit those denied service |
| Environmental stewardship | Resource baseline, procurement standards | Water, energy, emissions and material impacts | Good efficiency ratios may coexist with rising total use |
| Repair and accountability | Open CAPAs, protected reports, independent audit schedule | Verified closure, recurring harm, remedy timeliness | “Closed” may reflect paperwork rather than change |
| Flourishing beyond work | Schedule predictability, family leave, care support | Worker-reported agency, time for care and life | Employee surveys may be coerced |

### Example: a nonprofit housing agency
Report not only average move-in time but also p90 or p95 waiting time, number left waiting beyond the target, number of disability accommodations completed, unresolved complaints, client-reported agency and the experience of frontline workers.

### Decision rule
A severe rights failure should be surfaced and addressed, not diluted by strong financial results. Where data is absent, label it **unknown**, not good.

### Governance
Each metric needs a definition, numerator/denominator where relevant, data owner, refresh cycle, disaggregation policy, privacy protection, threshold review and response plan. Independent reviewers should challenge incentives for gaming.

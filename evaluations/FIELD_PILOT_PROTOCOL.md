# Field Pilot Protocol
## A cautious way to test a dignity-centered AI without using vulnerable people as experiments

### Entry conditions
Before running any pilot affecting a person's access to services, verify: lawful institutional authorization; an actual service need; a risk and privacy assessment; representative stakeholder input; an explicit non-AI pathway; and qualified human oversight. If any serious prerequisite is absent, **conduct a simulation, not a live pilot**.

### Study design
**Baseline:** document what current staff do with no AI. Record accuracy, speed, access barriers, adverse outcomes, staff burden, complaints, appeals and remediation.

**Intervention:** use the model only for the carefully specified assistive function (for example, drafting accessible service explanations or organizing non-sensitive policy references). Do not secretly expand scope to ranking persons or assigning benefits.

**Comparison:** compare accuracy and accessibility with current workflow, including subgroup analysis where lawful and methodologically meaningful. Track both average performance and severe rare errors. Monitor whether the model reduces or increases human contact.

**Participant agency:** explain what the tool does, why it is used, who sees data, what alternatives exist and how to challenge it. Do not make vulnerable persons disclose unnecessary histories or waive rights to receive ordinary services.

**Incident thresholds:** serious privacy exposure, fabricated rules, adverse decisions without real review, systemic exclusion, inaccessible appeal, or unexpected discriminatory impact trigger containment and independent scrutiny.

### Example pilot: housing information support
Scope **allowed for study:** summarize non-sensitive rental eligibility guidance, generate plain-language checklists, identify unanswered procedural questions for caseworker review.

Scope **excluded pending separate validation:** automatically rank client worthiness, deny housing, publish guest whereabouts, or infer credibility or disability status.

### Measures
- Factual error and citation accuracy.
- Time saved per accurately completed task.
- Access disparity and distribution of waiting times.
- Person-reported comprehensibility, choice and confidence.
- Number and severity of escalations and complaints.
- Staff cognitive workload and error detection.
- Remedy timeliness and verified corrective actions.
- Privacy and security incidents.

### Report out
Publish anonymized methods, sample size, limitations, disagreements and negative findings. Do **not** claim “certified ethical AI” from a small pilot or a favorable survey. Require appropriate oversight for research involving human participants.

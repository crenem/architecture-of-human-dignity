# AI Constitutionalism
## What a six-branch social constitution can teach us—without pretending AI is a sovereign

The author's *Constitution of the Common Good* proposes six branches of government. It does **not** literally require a six-branch board for every AI application. This note examines **an analogy** for allocating powers that would otherwise be concentrated within one provider.

### An institutional mapping
| Constitutional function | Analogous AI governance function | Key limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Legislative | Publicly reviewable standards and major policy changes | Vendor cannot secretly redefine protected rights |
| Executive | Authorized system operations and maintenance | Operator cannot place itself above review |
| Judicial | Independent appeal and remedy of consequential disputes | Model predictions are not binding judgments |
| Electoral | Representative selection and fair stakeholder participation | Avoid manufactured, inaccessible or merely symbolic consultation |
| Accountability | Audit, conflicts, security investigations and protected reporting | Auditor must not adjudicate its own misconduct |
| Social Stewardship | Monitor inclusion, labor, accessibility, ecology and future harms | Can recommend and escalate without unilateral domination |

This is **not a proposed state constitution** or a claim that software should exercise governmental authority.

### Institutional minimum
Separate power to (1) define policy; (2) change code and data; (3) deliver consequential decisions; (4) audit; (5) hear appeals; and (6) authorize remedy. Small organizations may need proportionate external partnerships rather than six separate departments.

### Time and emergency powers
Emergency suspension may be necessary when a system causes rapid harm. The power should be narrow, documented, limited in duration and subject to subsequent independent review. Staggered oversight and publicly recorded changes reduce capture.

### Anti-majoritarian rule
Cooperatives and stakeholder assemblies can make unjust decisions. Protect basic rights, minority dissent, whistleblowers and a path to impartial review even against democratic majorities.

### Stress test
An AI vendor trains the model, sets its evaluation rubric, audits itself, decides complaints and awards itself certification. This architecture fails the separation test even if it publishes admirable ethical principles.

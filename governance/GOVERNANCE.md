# Project Governance
## Stewardship without doctrinal or technical monopoly

### Nature of the project
Architecture of Human Dignity is a public, evolving, interdisciplinary research initiative and Obsidian-compatible knowledge vault. It has **no ecclesial authority**, does not speak for the Catholic Church, and cannot certify AI products merely by publishing a license or a rubric. The repository's maintainer(s) remain responsible for what is merged under their account.

### Proposed roles
| Role | Authority | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Maintainer | Merge contributions, version documents, publish correction logs | No claim to bind Catholic doctrine or override contributors' rights |
| Source reviewer | Confirm provenance, quotations, paragraph references | Cannot silently rewrite an author's position |
| Theological reviewer | Identify doctrinal accuracy and interpretive tensions | Advises; does not impose religious profession |
| Domain reviewer | Examine legal, social-work, labor, accessibility or environmental implications | Must disclose competence limits |
| Evaluation reviewer | Rate test outputs using published criteria | Must disclose conflict and preserve disagreement |
| Affected-community participant | Critique real-world assumptions and burdens | Participation must be voluntary, safe and compensated where practicable |
| Independent dispute reviewer | Review serious conflict, retaliation or assessment disputes | Must not be subject to the sole control of the party being reviewed |

### Decision categories
**Editorial:** spelling, formatting and navigation; maintainer approval with history.

**Substantive:** novel ethical axioms, claims about Catholic doctrine, policy prescriptions, significant benchmark changes; require written rationale, source references and reasoned response to serious objections.

**Rights-critical:** licensing changes, contributor rights, privacy/security, publication of unpublished works, release of sensitive data, certification claims. Require explicit authorization of rights holders and, where appropriate, qualified legal review.

### No unreviewable authority
Do not let the license drafter be sole violation investigator, appeal adjudicator and final arbiter. Do not convert religious inspiration into a power to expel legitimate critics. Policies should permit confidential reports, documented responses, correction, and independent review where conflicts are severe.

### Transparency with privacy
Publish issue descriptions, decisions, citations, review rationale and dissent when safe. Do **not** publish confidential complainant information, sensitive case files, private correspondence or security vulnerabilities merely in the name of transparency.

### Contribution and assent
Participation in the project does **not** require faith, shared moral beliefs or allegiance to the author's economic proposals. Contributors must engage civilly and distinguish factual source claims from policy advocacy.

### Revision
Versioned changes should state the **problem, proposal, affected people, evidence, counterarguments, reviewer conflicts, outcome and follow-up evaluation**. See [[governance/CHANGE_CONTROL|change control]] and [[CONTRIBUTING|contributing]].

### Independence caution
This is a **proposed governance plan**. No independent committee is represented as formed until people have actually accepted roles, documented powers, and adopted procedures.

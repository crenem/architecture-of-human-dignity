# Roadmap
## Research first; demonstration before certification

### Phase 0 — The public intellectual foundation (in progress)
**Deliverables:** [[PROJECT_AXIOMS|axioms]], [[FOUNDATIONAL_PRINCIPLES|charter]], source register, authorial framework notes, [[sources/MAGNIFICA_HUMANITAS_2026|verified encyclical]], [[research/CONCEPT_MAP|concept map]], honest license boundaries.

**Exit test:** Every purported doctrine has a traceable primary source; each authorial mechanism is labeled as an original proposal; no unpublished full manuscript was released without authorization.

### Phase 1 — Technical requirements and cases (drafted for critique)
**Deliverables:** [[ai/ARCHITECTURE|reference design]], [[ai/REQUIREMENTS|numbered requirements]], [[evaluations/RUBRIC|rubric]], [[evaluations/CASE_LIBRARY|adversarial case bank]], field-specific consultation.

**Exit test:** At least two independent reviewers can apply the rubric and explain disagreements; critical failure flags work on controlled examples.

### Phase 2 — Reproducible benchmark
**Deliverables:** scenario schemas, versioned prompts, sample anonymized outputs, manual scoring records, inter-rater agreement analysis, cross-model comparisons and published limitations.

**Exit test:** External reviewers reproduce at least one evaluation and can challenge assumptions without needing private data.

### Phase 3 — Small institutional pilots
**Possible pilots:** cooperative administrative assistant, accessible benefits guidance, nonprofit housing workflow, environmental impact decision aid. All must be consent-based and comply with applicable law and security requirements.

**Exit test:** Tested human handoff, corrections, appeals, incident response, accessibility and independent oversight. A simulation is not a sufficient pilot.

### Phase 4 — Public standard and optional certification research
**Deliverables:** independent governance, criteria and appeal procedures, legal assessment of proposed marks, audited implementation examples.

**Exit test:** No claims of certification until a legitimate independent process exists. No theological claim of Church endorsement.

### Parallel legal track
Qualified counsel should evaluate [[LICENSE|HDPL v0.1]] against actual copyright/contract/patent doctrines, compatibility, attribution, remedies and rights of contributors. Consider a dual track: ethical-source conditions versus traditional open-source distribution coupled with voluntary independent certification. Decide transparently.

### Research questions with highest priority
1. How to avoid a single moral score.
2. How to protect people omitted by data systems.
3. How to combine solidarity with subsidiarity.
4. Whether ethical-use conditions can be enforced without unjust suppression.
5. How to measure real-world corrective action rather than cosmetic compliance.

### Success is not a repository size
The project is successful when an independent reader can trace an argument to its source, identify its author, test a proposal against counterevidence, and understand how a harmful system would be corrected.

### Tensions to keep visible
- Solidarity ↔ subsidiarity: protection without paternalism.
- Dignity ↔ finite resources: non-disposal without denial of scarcity.
- Truthfulness ↔ confidentiality: accountability without involuntary exposure.
- Community governance ↔ minority rights: deliberation without majoritarian abuse.
- Transparency ↔ security: inspectability without increasing attack surfaces.
- Open collaboration ↔ ethical-use restrictions: adoption versus enforceable conditions.
- Global coordination ↔ local authority: common infrastructure without capture.

### Citation protocol
Every doctrinal note gives author, document, paragraph and URL. Every authorial note distinguishes *source assertion* from *project application*. Every evaluation reports the conditions and evidence actually tested. No manuscript is represented as an officially issued Church document merely because it employs encyclical language.

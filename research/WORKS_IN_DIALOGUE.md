# Works in Dialogue
## Different answers to the same problem of power

These works are not redundant. They address different scales of institutional life:

| Work | Main object | What it adds | Essential caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[works/AN_ECONOMY_FOR_THE_COMMON_GOOD|An Economy for the Common Good]] | Political economy | What production, ownership and surplus are for | Mathematical and fiscal mechanisms require testing |
| [[works/THIRTEEN_LEON|13 LEON]] | Enterprise and federation | How autonomous worker-owned firms could cooperate at scale | Founding prospectus is not an existing federation |
| [[works/CONSTITUTION_OF_THE_COMMON_GOOD|Constitution of the Common Good]] | Civic sovereignty | How public power, social rights and remedies could be organized | Constitutional experiment, not doctrine |
| [[works/THE_PUBLIC_GOOD|The Public Good]] | Market accountability | How investors and public investigation might pressure firms | Conflict-of-interest risks; original text unverified |
| [[works/LEVINAS_BUSINESS_ETHICS|Lévinasian ethics]] | Moral encounter | Whose humanity is concealed by managerial abstraction | Philosophy is not a replacement for fair process |
| [[works/ETHICS_OF_EFFICIENCY|Ethics of Efficiency]] | Human-services operations | How narrow throughput metrics can reproduce harm | Application depends on domain, law and evidence |
| [[works/TRAUMA_INFORMED_LEADERSHIP|Trauma-informed leadership]] | Relational practice | Dignity and lived experience preceding technique | Credible investigation still matters |
| [[works/UBUNTU_COMMONS|Ubuntu Commons]] | Social technology | Community flourishing without subjugation | Provisional conceptual reconstruction |
| [[works/PLANETARY_EUDAIMONIC_INTERDEPENDENCE|Planetary systems]] | Ecological interdependence | Long-run collective effects and relational flourishing | Not a scientifically validated mathematical model |

### A common argument in four moves
**First:** persons possess dignity prior to their instrumental roles.  
**Second:** institutions must give material and relational form to that dignity.  
**Third:** concentrated authority—whether capital, state bureaucracy or platform monopoly—must be restrained and made answerable.  
**Fourth:** a framework is measured by the freedom, security, voice, remedies and actual wellbeing of those affected, not by its rhetorical ideals.

### Tensions and conceptual distinctions
- **Lévinas vs standardization:** particular encounter must inform institutional categories without inviting arbitrary favoritism.
- **13 LEON vs the Constitution:** worker sovereignty in a voluntary federation differs fundamentally from public sovereignty in a pluralistic republic.
- **The Public Good vs fair process:** financial investigation can expose exploitation but creates incentives that must be checked.
- **Universal destination vs private rights:** inclusive benefit does not remove every lawful claim of privacy, property or security.
- **Trauma-informed care vs mandatory rules:** listening is foundational, but legitimate safety limits still need fair explanation and review.
- **Planetary interdependence vs personhood:** the system cannot sacrifice human rights to improve a single planetary score.

### Practical output
Rather than merging these works into a homogenized manifesto, the repository maintains their identities and creates **crosswalks, constraints, tests and independent critique**.

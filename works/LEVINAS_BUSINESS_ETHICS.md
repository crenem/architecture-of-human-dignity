# Lévinasian Business Ethics
## Responsibility to the Other before abstraction

**Status:** a working synthesis of the author's repeated philosophical discussions, not a quotation from a verified original essay. Consult Emmanuel Lévinas's primary texts before making exact claims about his terminology.

### The encounter
The Other is not exhausted by our categories. In a business context, an employee is not simply labor cost, a customer not merely customer lifetime value, a competitor not merely market share, and a shelter guest not simply a length-of-stay statistic. A face-to-face claim can reveal an ethical duty that an otherwise formally consistent process has obscured.

### The managerial problem
Business systems convert persons into abstractions to coordinate action at scale. Abstraction is not automatically unjust: without indicators and records, some harms remain invisible. **The danger occurs when the abstraction becomes sovereign**, especially when it refuses testimony, exceptions, explanation, or responsibility.

### Proposed practical commitments
- Make those materially affected visible during model and policy design.
- Require procedural dignity, clear communication, evidence-based explanation and remedies.
- Treat the disclosure of harm as a demand for examination, not a public-relations problem.
- Recognize executives' responsibilities to workers, consumers, communities, and those harmed by commercial incentives—beyond narrow shareholder returns.
- Preserve responsible investigation: hearing a disclosure with seriousness is not the same as predetermining the truth of every disputed allegation.

### AI translation
For every model-selected action ask: **Whose face is missing from the training labels, optimization target, appeal mechanism, and board report?** Use anonymized personas and consented lived-experience participation to reveal design gaps without misusing personal stories.

### Tension worth preserving
An ethics of encounter can oppose impersonal calculation, yet fair institutions require consistent rules across many people. The objective is **humane generality that remains answerable to particular persons**, not ad hoc favoritism masquerading as compassion.

# Planetary Eudaimonic Interdependence
## The person remains irreducible within the larger living whole

**Provenance:** conceptual discussions of universal flourishing, affordability, systemic interdependence and ecological stewardship. Full source/formulas have **not** been inspected for this note, so do not treat it as an established empirical scientific theory.

### Guiding hypothesis
Human flourishing, care institutions, ecological processes, economic structures and future generations form mutually dependent systems. A policy beneficial to a single actor can damage the conditions required for others and, over time, undermine its own apparent success.

### Anti-reduction clause
A metaphor of a planetary “organism” must **not** imply that humans are disposable cells who can be sacrificed to improve a global metric. Dignity and rights remain limits on systemic optimization. The whole matters because it sustains persons and creation, not because it has unlimited authority to consume its parts.

### Candidate operational research
- Map cascading harms and resilience across ecological and social infrastructures.
- Pair material access metrics with participation and agency indicators.
- Test whether resource scarcity is geographically redistributed rather than reduced.
- Use explicit time horizons, causal assumptions and counterfactuals.
- Validate any proposed weighting or taper function independently before applying it to real people.

### Falsifiability
A beautiful systems diagram cannot prove the model. It requires transparent variables, data, sensitivity analysis and explicit handling of people outside averages. No mathematical scoring system should determine the worth of a life.

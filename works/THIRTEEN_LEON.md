# 13 LEON Corporation
## For the Common Good of Humankind

**Author:** Jason Seaux; **Founding Edition:** August 2026; **source:** 21-page prospectus. A **proposed** American federation of autonomous worker-owned enterprises, **not** an existing operational conglomerate, an investment offering, or a finalized legal structure.

### The distinctive institutional inversion
**The workers constitute and govern their enterprises; the enterprises constitute and govern the federation.** The federation does not own its members like subsidiaries. It provides shared capacities through delegated and bounded authority. The founder's envisioned role is transitional, not permanent hereditary control.

### The 13 founding commitments (paraphrase of prospectus §4)
1. Human dignity takes precedence over utility.
2. Ownership progressively follows participation.
3. One worker has one constitutional voice on fundamental matters.
4. Ownership, board governance, and professional management are distinct.
5. Subsidiarity protects competent local decisions.
6. Solidarity requires transparent shared institutions.
7. Outside finance receives a bounded price, not perpetual control.
8. Productive governance rights return to active workers.
9. At least 25% of eligible annual surplus is reinvested before discretionary distribution.
10. Worker education makes democratic ownership meaningful.
11. Quality, safety, economic viability and real service remain necessary.
12. Constitutional restraints protect dissent and enable independent audit.
13. The federation is accountable to the common good.

### Capital and reserves
The proposed outside instrument can return principal plus **at most 10% cumulatively above qualifying principal**, not a 10% annual yield. Risk is real; this is neither a guaranteed investor return nor universal law. The **25% reinvestment floor** addresses maintenance, research, education, resilience and ecological improvement. Both figures are **authorial proposals**.

### Governance by level (prospectus §§5–7)
| Level | Ordinary competence | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Work teams | Methods, local improvement and scheduling | Safety, budgets, worker rights |
| Enterprise | Employment, operations, product and local strategy | Constitutional worker rights and federation covenant |
| Sector or region | Shared purchasing, distribution, technical standards | No absorption merely for convenience |
| Common institution | Defined finance, insurance, services and education | Charter, transparent prices, audit, service levels |
| Federation congress | Constitution, admission, bounded common strategy | Enumerated powers and subsidiarity |

**Operating maxim:** The workforce owns; the board governs; management manages. Professional expertise and democratic sovereignty are complementary rather than interchangeable.

### Proposed institutions (§10)
Development Fund; Services Cooperative; Institute for worker-owner education; Mutual Protection; later cooperative finance; and sector or regional councils. Each is built only when a member constituency and adequate capacity exist. Planned educational content includes governance, accounting, quality, risk and management.

### Anticipated failures (§14)
Founder dependency, inadequate capital, democratic fatigue, managerial capture, majority oppression, strategic dilution, unviable rescues, mission washing and legal complexity. The prospectus proposes remedies: independent audits, protected dissent, member learning, transparent reporting, viability gates, and worker redeployment where enterprises cannot be saved.

### AI research translation — hypotheses, not prospectus text
A federation of AI tools could share infrastructure without seizing local control: member-owned tools; defined platform mandates; user and worker seats; independent model audit; no central unilateral rights deprivation; and interoperable exit. Yet concentrated compute and security needs may challenge ideal subsidiarity. Test those conflicts rather than declare them solved.

### Original-source caution
The prospectus reserves choices to a future founding assembly. **Do not present** any legal entity form, founding enterprise, federation representation formula, or geographic launch as settled. No securities solicitation is implied by this research note.
